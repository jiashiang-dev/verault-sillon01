SILLON 01 / TECHNICAL DOSSIER

SILLON01

A hand-wound time-only watch design, defined by a shallow line in each lug and a paired mark at twelve.

Sillon 01 is a fictional portfolio concept. No watch has been manufactured, measured, certified or offered for sale. Dimensions below are design values or targets; the Sellita movement is a proposed base.

Explore the watch↗
01 / FORM

Measured restraint.

A compact round steel case with short, downward-contoured lugs. Four fine incisions are cut into the lug tops, one per lug. They stop before the strap gaps.

Case diameter
38.5 mm, excluding the crown — design dimension.
Lug tip to lug tip
45.8 mm — design dimension.
Total thickness
9.5 mm design target, subject to CAD packaging and a physical prototype.
Lug width
20.0 mm with conventional spring bars.
Case
Natural 316L stainless steel. Lengthwise fine satin upper planes, horizontal satin flank and a nominal 0.6 mm visible polished chamfer.
Signature line
One centered longitudinal channel on each lug top, nominally 0.35 mm wide and 0.12 mm deep. It ends before the strap gap; it does not cross the bezel, crystal or dial.
Bezel and crystal
Fixed, low-profile steel bezel with circular satin top. Slight box sapphire front crystal, rising approximately 0.6 mm above the bezel, with inner anti-reflective coating.
Crown
Unguarded at 3 o’clock, 5.8 mm nominal diameter, fine axial knurling, push-pull action and a plain circular end cap.
Water resistance
50 m / 5 bar is an engineering target only. No assembled watch has been tested or rated.
02 / DIAL

Made to read.

The surface stays quiet so the hands and indices can do their work. The two marks at twelve give the watch its second, smaller signature.

Construction
Two-level dial: restrained radial graphite brushing at the centre and a recessed, deeper-graphite peripheral minute track.
Minute track
Sixty cool-grey engraved marks. Every fifth mark is slightly longer; no numerals.
Hour indices
Two equal, separate applied steel batons at twelve, with eleven single indices elsewhere. The 3, 6 and 9 markers are subtly longer.
Hands
Three central hands. Lightly tapered steel hour and minute blades; a fine central seconds needle reaches the minute track. Its final approximately 2.2 mm has muted clay colour.
Dial text
Small branding is treated with restraint. No date, small-seconds subdial or certification text is part of the design.
03 / MOVEMENT

A daily ritual.

The proposed movement is a sourced manual-wind calibre, viewed through a sapphire caseback at its real scale. Winding is the daily interaction around which the watch is designed.

Proposed base
Sellita SW210-1 b, no-date manual-wind configuration with central sweep seconds and stop-seconds.
Frequency
28,800 vibrations per hour / 4 Hz.
Jewels
18.
Power reserve
Typically 45 hours according to Sellita’s catalogue; this is not a measured performance guarantee for Sillon 01.
Movement dimensions
25.60 mm diameter and 3.35 mm height for the proposed calibre.
Presentation
Rhodium-tone finishing is proposed, subject to the actual sourced movement. A substantial steel retaining ring surrounds the movement under the sapphire exhibition window. There is no rotor.

The movement figures above come from Sellita’s published catalogue. Case fit, finishing availability and performance of a complete watch require physical validation.

04 / MATERIALS

One configuration.

Steel, graphite and leather provide the contrast. Light does the rest.

Strap
Dark umber calf leather with visible natural grain, warm contrast stitching and a clean folded edge. It tapers from 20 mm at the lugs to 18 mm at the buckle.
Buckle
Brushed steel pin buckle with a narrow polished chamfer and a VÉRAULT wordmark on its face.
Material tones
Graphite dial, natural steel, dark umber leather and a muted clay seconds tip. Screen colours are art-direction references, not manufacturing tolerances.
05 / STATUS

Designed, not produced.

This dossier describes a product design for a professional portfolio project. It is not a commercial specification, manufacturer’s warranty, test report or offer to purchase.

Before a real watch could make production claims, its case package, stem height, clearances, water resistance, movement sourcing and material finishes would require engineering and prototype validation. No complete-watch origin claim is made.
