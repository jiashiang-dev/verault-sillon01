A clear account of the sources and behavior behind the Sillon 01 experience.

VÉRAULT and Sillon 01 are fictional. The images visualize a proposed watch; they are not photographs of a manufactured, tested or certified product.

01 / CREDITS

The work.

The product imagery was supplied by the project owner for this portfolio concept. No third-party watch or lifestyle stock photography appears in the launch story.

Product concept
VÉRAULT — Sillon 01, a fictional contemporary mechanical watch design.
Imagery
Ten supplied Sillon 01 concept visualizations, displayed in the launch experience with responsive crops. Original PNG files are retained alongside optimized WebP versions.
Interaction
The Hero adapts an existing 21st.dev parallax component. Scroll motion uses GSAP ScrollTrigger; desktop smooth scrolling uses Lenis.
Typography
A system sans-serif and system monospace fallback. No unlicensed commercial font file is embedded.
Movement reference
The proposed Sellita SW210-1 b specifications are attributed to Sellita’s catalogue. VÉRAULT does not claim to manufacture the movement.
02 / ACCESSIBILITY

The whole story stays available.

Motion adds depth to the imagery, while copy, specifications and navigation remain in normal document flow.

Reduced motion
When the system preference requests less motion, scroll-linked parallax and desktop smooth scrolling are disabled. Static images preserve the same content and order.
Keyboard
The mobile menu, material selector, navigation links and dossier actions are available by keyboard, with visible focus states.
Images
Product and lifestyle images have descriptive alternative text. Decorative type layers are hidden from assistive technology.
Feedback
If an image, interaction or section is difficult to use, the portfolio owner should be contacted through the portfolio where this concept is presented.
