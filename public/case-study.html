INDEPENDENT PORTFOLIO PROJECT / VÉRAULT

A line made tangible.

Sillon 01 explores how a watch, its image and its website can speak in the same precise visual language.

VÉRAULT is a fictional contemporary independent watch concept. This is a design case study, not the story of an operating watchmaker or a manufactured product.

01 / THE PREMISE

Begin with the object.

The brief was to make a fictional watch launch credible enough for a flagship web design portfolio project. The product’s form and mechanics had to be coherent before motion, typography or imagery could carry them.

One signature.

A shallow channel sits on each lug. Two separate batons at twelve echo its paired-line identity. The motif is a physical detail first, a graphic device second.

One movement logic.

The concept is hand-wound, time-only and no-date, with three central hands. The proposed base is a Sellita SW210-1 b. No small seconds or rotor appears in the product design.

One product brief.

Front, side, back, campaign, macro and wrist views share the Sillon 01 proportions, graphite dial, steel case and umber strap. Final production imagery would require shot-to-shot verification of markings and finishing.

02 / PRODUCT DESIGN

The detail earns its place.

A 38.5 mm steel case and short 45.8 mm lug span keep the proposed watch modest on a wrist. Satin planes stay subdued; a narrow polished edge describes the shape only when light reaches it.

03 / WEBSITE NARRATIVE

Seven quiet chapters.

The site moves from the watch as an object to the details that make its scale and daily use intelligible. Each chapter has one reason to exist and, at most, one visual event.

Form and reading.

The Hero establishes the model. A brief case reveal finds the lug line. A still dial chapter gives the paired twelve and minute track time to be inspected.

Ritual and material.

The crown and proposed movement explain hand winding. Steel, sapphire and leather are then available as user-selected close details, with factual captions.

Scale and resolution.

Daylight wrist imagery returns the watch to human proportion. The final chapter settles into a product portrait and a real technical dossier, without a false purchase action.

04 / INTERACTION

Motion has a job.

The five-layer Hero uses small differences in travel to separate typography and product. A short profile reveal helps the eye locate the milled line. Dial, wrist and closing chapters remain mostly still so the images can be studied.

Mobile has its own product composition and reduced scroll intensity. With reduced motion enabled, the site preserves the same content in static document flow and does not require animation to reveal a fact or action.

05 / PRODUCTION STATUS

The honest frame.

The launch site uses supplied product and lifestyle visualizations. They show the design intention, while the physical watch remains a fictional portfolio concept rather than a manufactured object.

The renders make the imagined object tangible. They are visual studies, not evidence of a tested case, produced movement package or certified origin.

The website preserves the manual-wind story and identifies movement details as a proposed specification. Product and material details remain subject to physical validation.
